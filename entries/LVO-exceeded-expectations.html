Las Vegas Open Day Two Round Up!

October 3rd, 2026

Day two of the Las Vegas Open is in the books, and the halls never slowed down. Tables were packed from the first dice roll in the morning to the last round at night. Here's a look at the biggest moments from the tournament floor, the hobby areas and beyond.

Tournament highlights

The Warhammer 40,000 Championships hit the halfway point, and only a handful of players remain undefeated. Steve Anderson and their Black Templars stole the show on the top table with a last-turn comeback that had the crowd on its feet.

Beyond the tables

The hobby classes were standing-room only, with painters of every level picking up new techniques. The vendor hall stayed busy all day, and the event-exclusive miniatures at the Warhammer pop-up store were a hot item once again.

As the sun went down, attendees spilled out into Las Vegas for dinner, meetups and a well-earned break. Narrative players wrapped up another chapter of their campaign, setting the stage for a dramatic finish.

Coming up tomorrow

The final day decides it all. Top players will battle for championship titles, and the awards ceremony will crown this year's winners. Check back for the full wrap-up.
