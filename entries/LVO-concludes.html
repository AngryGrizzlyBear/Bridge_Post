Las Vegas Open Concludes

October 4th, 2026

After four days of dice, paint and very little sleep, the Las Vegas Open has come to a close. Champions were crowned, armies were packed up, and thousands of players headed home with new friends and a few more miniatures than they arrived with. Here's how it all wrapped up.

Your Champion

The Warhammer 40,000 final came down to the wire. Steve Anderson and their Black Templars edged out Mike Wilson in a nail-biter decided on the last turn, claiming the championship title.

Awards beyond the win column

Winning isn't everything at LVO. Best Painted went to Nickolas Cage for a stunning Necrons that drew a crowd all weekend. Best Sportsman honors went to Mike Landar, voted by fellow players, and the Narrative campaign wrapped with Space Marines claiming victory in the story.

Final thoughts

What stood out most this year was the community. Opponents shook hands, swapped tips and cheered each other on, even in the tensest games. That spirit is what keeps people coming back to Vegas year after year.

See you next year

The next Las Vegas Open is expected around fall 2027. Keep an eye on lasvegasopen.net for dates and ticket alerts. Start painting now!
