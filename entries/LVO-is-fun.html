LVO is Fun!

October 2nd, 2026

If you play Warhammer or any miniature wargame, the Las Vegas Open (LVO) belongs on your bucket list. It calls itself the biggest tabletop gaming event in the world, and it packs four days of tournaments, painting, shopping and socializing into one very loud, very bright weekend.

Think of it as a pilgrimage for the hobby. Thousands of players fly in from around the globe, armies in hand, to roll dice in Sin City. Here's why it's worth the trip.

A tournament for whatever you play

LVO runs events for nearly every Games Workshop system: Warhammer 40,000, Age of Sigmar, Kill Team, Warhammer Underworlds, The Horus Heresy, Legions Imperialis, The Old World and Blood Bowl. Non-GW games like Star Wars: Legion and Marvel Crisis Protocol get their own brackets too. The 40K Championships draw some of the best players on the planet, so top-table games are worth watching even if you're not in contention. But you don't need to be a tournament shark to have fun. Plenty of people come simply to play a lot of games against opponents they'd never meet at their local store.

Not into competitive play? No problem

LVO is one of the best places anywhere to dive into narrative Warhammer. Story-driven campaign events let you forge a legend for your army without stressing over win rates. Painters are well served too. Hobby classes run all weekend, from beginner sessions to master-level tutorials, so you can sharpen your brushwork between games and head home with new techniques.

Bring an empty suitcase

The vendor hall is huge, with exhibitors from around the world selling terrain, dice, paints, bits and accessories you rarely see in person. Budget accordingly. Games Workshop usually sends its Warhammer Events team with a travelling pop-up store stocked with event-exclusive miniatures and merch. LVO has also hosted major Warhammer preview reveals in past years, and attendees get to see them before the rest of the internet does.

The people are the real draw

Ask LVO regulars what they love most and many will say the community. Meetups, creator hangouts and special events keep the energy high between rounds. It's a rare chance to meet the YouTubers and podcasters you follow, swap painting tips with strangers, and finally play the online friends you've only argued tactics with in Discord.

Easy logistics, and then there's Vegas

And when the last round ends, you're in Las Vegas. Food, shows and nightlife are right outside the door, which makes LVO easy to turn into a vacation with your gaming group.

Planning your trip

LVO moved from January to the fall in 2025 and expanded into a larger venue. The 2026 edition ran October 1 - 4, so expect the next one around fall 2027. Tickets for 2026 went on sale in April, and popular events fill up, so sign up for ticket alerts at lasvegasopen.net well ahead of time. Whether you come to compete, paint, shop or just soak in the atmosphere, LVO is the kind of weekend that reminds you why you got into the hobby. See you at the tables.
